## Full Summary

This experiment implements a rigorous five-model comparison for coffee cooling temperature trajectories across four published datasets (Kurzweg 1988, Manitoba workshop, Ferreira 2024 cup, Lomen & Lovelock). Models tested: B0 (Newton single-exponential baseline, 2 parameters), B1 (piecewise two-exponential, 5 parameters), C1 (coupled mass-energy ODE with evaporation, 3 parameters), C2 (Bayesian change-point MAP estimate, 3 parameters), C3 (flux-crossover with evaporation suppression, 3 parameters, tau sweep 3-8 min). Each dataset is evaluated with four split ratios (70%, 75%, 80%, 85%) yielding 16 total examples. Metrics computed: BIC (n ln(RSS/n) + 2k), out-of-sample RMSE, and predicted time-to-drinkable (50°C crossing). Results: C3 wins BIC on 3/4 datasets at 80% split; C2 achieves lowest OOS RMSE on all 4; all models agree within ~1-2 min on drinkable time.
